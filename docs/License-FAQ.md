# License FAQ

### Can you summarize what Companion Home Assistant Voice PE's license allows?

The license grants free rights to use, modify, and redistribute Companion Home Assistant Voice PE source code for personal or nonprofit purposes. The intent is to stay permissive for those uses while limiting commercial abuse of Companion Intelligence products.

Companion Home Assistant Voice PE connects a [Home Assistant Voice Preview Edition](https://www.home-assistant.io/voice_control/voice_remote_local_assistant/) device to Companion Intelligence: a Python `voice-bridge` speaks the open Wyoming protocol to the Voice PE hardware, and relays audio to a TypeScript `ci-server` façade that exposes an OpenAI-compatible API in front of a local Ollama model.

Companion Home Assistant Voice PE uses the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0). The full text is in [`LICENSE.md`](../LICENSE.md).

### What freedoms do you have as a Companion Home Assistant Voice PE user?

If you use Companion Home Assistant Voice PE for personal or nonprofit purposes, you can view, modify, and redistribute the source under the same license terms, subject to the PolyForm Noncommercial restrictions.

### Can I fork Companion Home Assistant Voice PE?

Yes. For noncommercial use, you can fork, modify, and redistribute Companion Home Assistant Voice PE under the same license.

No. Selling Companion Home Assistant Voice PE, including cloud-hosted instances, appliances that ship it, or paid support for those products, is not permitted. For commercial partnership questions, email partner@companionintelligence.com.

### Does this license apply to the rest of Companion Intelligence?

Companion Portal stays a separate commercial product — this license does not apply to it.

Companion Hub, Companion Memory, Companion Marketplace, Companion OS, Companion Capture, Companion Browser Extension, Companion OMI, Companion Mentra, Companion Even Realities, and Companion Pebble use the same PolyForm Noncommercial terms in their own repositories.
